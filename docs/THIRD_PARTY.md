# Third-party materials and license review

**Pending legal/source review before public release.**

- **Qt 6**: Qt is a third-party toolkit with licensing that varies by modules and distribution. Confirm the license terms and packaging obligations for the exact Qt components used.
- **DevilutionX**: a separate third-party game-engine project. Its upstream repository presently identifies its source as subject to the **Sustainable Use License** and non-commercial conditions. Do not copy its source into VibePedigree and apply MIT to it; obtain and retain upstream LICENSE and copyright notices for any independent engine derivative.
- **Diablo**: Blizzard's underlying game assets and trademarks are not owned by the VibePedigree author and are not included.
- **KinMap and local family records**: source and data ownership and privacy rights need separate review. Personal genealogy records must not be uploaded as part of a public source release.
- **`VibePedigree-Test2` WAV sounds and other assets**: provenance not established by the archive alone; exclude unless ownership or licensed redistribution can be documented.
- **Code contributions**: audit any files copied from third parties. The MIT copyright notice in this package applies only to material for which the named author has licensing authority.

A permissive license facilitates software use by people and coding tools, but cannot waive third-party restrictions or guarantee inclusion in model training datasets.
